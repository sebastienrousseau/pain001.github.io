ISO 20022 Roadmap: Every Deadline Through 2028

The migration was never a cliff — it is a schedule that runs to 2028. Every mandate, dated and sourced, updated each Standards Release.

3 min read Last reviewed 26 July 2026

The migration was never a cliff — it is a schedule. MT–MX coexistence ended in November 2025 and the next enforcement date is 14 November 2026, but the mandates keep landing through 2028: investigations, statements, direct debits, purpose codes, payee verification. This page is the living schedule, updated with each SWIFT Standards Release and re-verified against primary sources — last review 26 July 2026.

01. Done — the rails are ISO-native#

Date Milestone
Aug 2022 Singapore MEPS+ migrates (like-for-like)
Mar 2023 Eurosystem T2 goes live ISO-native; SWIFT CBPR+ coexistence begins
Jun 2023 Bank of England CHAPS migrates
Mar 2024 EBA CLEARING RT1 upgrades to the 2019 ISO version
Apr 2024 Hong Kong CHATS and US CHIPS migrate
9 Jan 2025 EU Instant Payments Regulation: euro-area PSPs must receive instant payments
14 Jul 2025 US Fedwire cuts over to ISO 20022 in a single day, 4,700+ participants
9 Oct 2025 IPR: euro-area PSPs must send instant payments and offer Verification of Payee on all SEPA credit transfers
Nov 2025 Japan BOJ-NET completes migration
22 Nov 2025 SWIFT MT–MX coexistence ends — MT 1xx/2xx/9xx retired for cross-border payment instructions; ~97% of instructions now ISO 20022

02. 14 November 2026 — the data-quality deadline#

Two enforcement events land on the same day:

  • Fully unstructured postal addresses are rejected in CBPR+ payments at network level. Structured — or hybrid (structured town and country plus up to two 70-character address lines) — becomes the floor. The fix lives in your customer master data, not your bank connection.
  • The interbank MT101 relay retires, replaced by pain.001 version 9. Multi-transaction MT101s are rejected outright; single-transaction messages get temporary contingency conversion.

Pain001 ships both requirements today: structured and hybrid address elements in generated XML, and one-command MT101 conversion. Full sourcing in the 2026 briefing.

03. 2027 — enrichment becomes mandatory#

Date Milestone Who it binds
9 Jul 2027 Verification of Payee and instant-payment obligations extend to non-euro-area EU PSPs (Regulation (EU) 2024/886) PSPs in non-euro member states
Nov 2027 Bank of England: purpose codes mandatory on ALL CHAPS payments (in channels within direct participants' control) CHAPS participants and their corporates
Nov 2027 CBPR+ exceptions & investigations migrate: MT195/295/199/299/999 retire in favour of camt.110/111 case management; banks must be able to receive camt.052/053/054 Financial institutions
End 2027 CPMI target for harmonised ISO 20022 data requirements in cross-border payments Market infrastructures globally

What it means for initiation: purpose codes and richer party data stop being optional enrichment and start being gate conditions. Files generated with complete structured data in 2026 pass unchanged in 2027.

04. 2028 — the long tail closes#

Date Milestone
Nov 2028 MT9xx statements retire — camt.05x becomes the only statement format
Nov 2028 MT104/107 direct debits and MT204 retire in favour of pain.008/pacs.003/pacs.010
Nov 2028 Charges messages move to camt.105/106

What it means: reconciliation pipelines still parsing MT940/MT942 have a hard end date. Pain001's camt.053 parser covers the receive side today.

05. No date, steady pressure#

  • pain.001 v10–v12 adoption — market infrastructures standardised on v9; later versions arrive channel by channel. Pain001 generates all ten versions and migrates records between them, so a version switch is a parameter, not a project.
  • LEIs in payments — 3 million+ active LEIs; already mandatory in scoped CHAPS payments, recommended in CBPR+ party identification.
  • Agentic payments — Mastercard, Visa, Google (AP2), and the Linux Foundation's x402 are building agent-authorisation rails; the payment-file layer they ultimately need is the one pain001-mcp already serves.

How to use this page#

Subscribe to the RSS feed for updates, check the "last reviewed" date above before relying on any deadline, and treat 2027–2028 items as scheduled but revisable — SWIFT has moved dates before, and each annual Standards Release can adjust the tail. Every date here traces to a primary source (SWIFT, Bank of England, European Commission, Federal Reserve, ECB, CPMI) cited in the 2026 briefing's reference list.
