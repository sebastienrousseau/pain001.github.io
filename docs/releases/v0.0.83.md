## Highlights ⭐️

* **In lockstep with pain001 0.0.83**: the website ships the suite's version with the core, MCP, LSP, loaders, mockbank, scaffold and native extension, one step after joining at 0.0.82.
* **Signature verification documented**: the reference, installation and compatibility pages describe `pain001 verify-signature`, `verify_signature` with a trust anchor, RSA-PSS and ECDSA signing with `--xml-sign-alg`, and the `sign` extra. The subcommand table also gains `pain001 check`.
* **Current library assets**: corpus pages, downloads, demo samples and the hash-pinned browser wheel are regenerated from the released pain001 0.0.83 library; apart from the version they name, they are the same as for 0.0.82.
