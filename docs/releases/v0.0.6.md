# pain001.com v0.0.6

Version 0.0.6 is the PRISM v2 release: the site redesigned for institutional
buyers, a photograph on every page, and the 2026 briefing corrected against
its primary sources after Swift deferred the structured-address deadline on
27 August 2026. It also adds the gates that would have caught what this
release fixed.

## What changed for readers

- **The deadline is right, in 35 languages.** The homepage ribbon, the
  briefing, the roadmap, the executive brief and every translation say that
  Swift deferred the November 2026 changes and will set new timing by
  December; the Fedwire release moved to November 2027; CHAPS purpose codes
  become mandatory in November 2027.
- **A new look.** One typeface, a cobalt-on-warm-grey palette of its own,
  distinct light and dark themes, and 155 photographs used once each.
- **Reference pages that read.** Every article page with two or more
  sections has a contents rail beside the text; titles no longer wrap three
  to six lines; wide tables scroll instead of crushing a column.
- **The demo, deep-linked.** Every scenario page opens its example in the
  browser demo, which now reads the link as a fragment so a crawler sees
  one URL for the demo, not 252.
- **Search in your language.** The search widget follows the page language,
  and the last English strings on translated pages are translated.
- **A real 404 page**, served with a 404 status.

## What changed for the site's operators

- **Gates.** A weekly check of the dated regulatory claims against their
  primary sources; a reading-measure audit of every page at four widths in
  real Chrome; a contrast gate at 7:1 in sRGB and Display P3; a site-wide
  WAVE-rule and axe AAA scan in light and dark; Lighthouse at 100 in all four
  categories on 13 routes across mobile, tablet, desktop, 4K and 8K, with a
  performance-only 99 measured a second time.
- **Performance.** The theme script is inlined under a CSP hash instead of
  being a render-blocking request; the French pages no longer fetch 36 KB of
  latin-ext Inter for one glyph; the tag list is a grid.
- **Search Console.** The soft 404 (`/404/` served with a 200), the 41
  `/try/?sample=` alternates and the `/index.html` duplicates are fixed at
  their causes; the eight dead documentation URLs are redirected in the
  library's docs.
- **CDN.** A Cloudflare page rule had cached every page at the edge for seven
  days, hiding deploys; the edge now follows the origin's ten-minute
  `max-age`, and the README records the rule.
- **CodeQL** alerts on the branch: 21 to 0.

The complete list is in `CHANGELOG.md` under 0.0.6.

## Verify this release

The assets are `pain001-site-v0.0.6.tar.gz`, its CycloneDX SBOM
`pain001-site-v0.0.6.cdx.json`, and `SHA256SUMS`. Build provenance and the
SBOM are attested; check them with:

```sh
gh attestation verify pain001-site-v0.0.6.tar.gz --repo sebastienrousseau/pain001.github.io
sha256sum --check SHA256SUMS
```

The checksums are appended below once the assets are published.
