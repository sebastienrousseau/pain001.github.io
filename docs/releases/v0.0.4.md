# pain001.com v0.0.4

Version 0.0.4 is a release-engineering fix. The site itself is unchanged from
v0.0.3; this release exists so that the v0.0.3 content reaches a published
release with its archive, checksums, SBOM and attestations intact.

What went wrong:

- the v0.0.3 tag built, packaged and attested provenance successfully, then
  failed at the SBOM attestation with `Error: SBOM file not found`, which
  skipped the publish step;
- the cause was `actions/attest-sbom`: it expands globs in `subject-path` but
  not in `sbom-path`. The same `dist/pain001-site-v*.tar.gz` pattern resolved
  for the subject in that very step, while `dist/pain001-site-v*.cdx.json`
  did not resolve for the SBOM;
- the SBOM was present and correct throughout. It is generated by ssg and
  published at <https://pain001.com/sbom.cdx.json>.

The fix:

- the packaging step exports `RELEASE_VERSION` to `GITHUB_ENV`, and the SBOM
  attestation names both files in full rather than by pattern.

Tags are immutable, so v0.0.3 keeps its tag and its provenance attestation
without a published release, and the fix ships here instead of re-pointing it.
