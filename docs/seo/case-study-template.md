# Case study template

Publish a case study only with the adopter's written permission, and only
with figures they supply and agree to publish. Nothing here is filled in
by default.

## Permission request (email)

> Subject: Could we name you as a pain001 user?
>
> Hello {name},
>
> Thank you for using pain001 at {organisation}. We would like to
> describe how you use it on pain001.com, in a short case study you
> approve word for word before anything is published. It would cover the
> payment flows you generate, the problem it replaced, and any figure you
> are happy to share, such as files per month or rejections avoided.
> If you would rather appear only as a logo, or not at all, that is
> equally fine.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute call?

## Page structure

1. **Who:** organisation, sector, country, and the team that runs it.
2. **Before:** how payment files were produced, and what went wrong.
3. **What they use:** which pain001 components, message versions and
   schemes (for example `pain.001.001.09` under CBPR+).
4. **Results:** only figures the adopter supplies and approves, each
   with its time period.
5. **Quote:** one sentence, attributed, approved.
6. **Links:** the adopter's site, and the pain001 pages they relied on.
