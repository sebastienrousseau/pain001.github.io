**Bug reports and feature requests** — [GitHub issues](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/pain001/issues) for the core, or the matching repository for [pain001-mcp](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/pain001-mcp), [pain001-lsp](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/pain001-lsp), and the loaders. Include your package version (`pain001 --version`), the message type, and a minimal reproducing input with **real account data removed**.

**Security disclosures** — please use [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/pain001/security) rather than a public issue. Coordinated disclosure is welcomed and credited. A machine-readable policy is published at [/security.txt](/security.txt).

**Everything else** — email [contact@pain001.com](mailto:contact@pain001.com), or find the maintainer at [sebastienrousseau.com](https://sebastienrousseau.com).

This is an open-source project, not a vendor helpdesk: issues are triaged in public, and well-written reports with reproductions get fixed fastest.
